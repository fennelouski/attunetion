Attunetion support

Updated October 1, 2026. Developer: Nathan Fennel.

Writing intentions

You can write and style daily, weekly, and monthly intentions on your device without an account or an AI connection. Your intentions and settings are stored locally.

Using AI

AI themes and generated suggestions require an internet connection and your agreement to sharing the relevant text with OpenAI through the Attunetion service. The consent screen explains what is sent. Choose Not Now to continue using manual writing and styling.

In Settings > Suggested Intentions, Stop Sharing with AI blocks future AI requests and turns off automatic suggestions. It cannot recall a request already sent. If the service is unavailable or reports a limit, continue writing manually and try AI again later.

Reminders and data

Manage reminders in the app and notification access in system settings. Delete Local Data in Settings removes your intentions, profile and consent, preferences, feedback, custom themes, widget copies, and reminders from this device. Bundled themes remain. When iCloud sync is enabled, record deletions may propagate to your other devices. Backups and information previously sent to service providers follow their own retention policies.

Get help

Contact Nathan Fennel with the app version, device and system version, the action you tried, and any error message. Do not include private intention text unless it is needed to explain the problem.
