Attunetion privacy policy

Updated November 22, 2026. Developer: Nathan Fennel.

Writing and storage on your device

Attunetion stores your intentions, personal profile text, preferences, custom themes, feedback, and AI consent choice on your device. You can write and style intentions without using AI. Widgets use a shared copy of local app data, and reminders use your notification choices. Versions with iCloud sync enabled can copy these records through Apple’s private CloudKit database across devices using the same Apple Account. Device backups may retain app data under your Apple settings.

Optional AI features

Before using AI, the app asks you to agree to sending text through the Attunetion service to OpenAI. Choosing Not Now leaves writing and manual styling available. The information sent depends on the feature:

  • AI themes send the intention text you are editing.
  • The manual AI generator sends the description you enter and the date used to generate suggestions. It does not attach your saved profile or intention history.
  • Automatic suggestions, when enabled, send your saved profile and up to ten previous intentions, including their text, dates, and day, week, or month scope.

The service uses this information to generate suggestions or appearance settings. Avoid including information you do not want processed by these services. Text may contain personal information even though the app does not require an online account.

Connection data and provider retention

The app may check service availability when it opens, even if you do not use AI. The current app API is hosted on Vercel. Amazon Web Services serves the marketing and policy pages and receives connection information when you visit. Requests expose connection information such as your IP address, request time, and route to the host. The service keeps IP addresses and request timestamps in memory to limit AI requests within a two-hour window. Expired entries are removed as subsequent requests run; an idle server instance may retain entries longer. This window is not a deletion deadline for hosting or provider logs.

The AI routes do not maintain a database of your submitted intentions. Their application error logs omit submitted text and provider response bodies. Vercel may retain request, location derived from IP address, and diagnostic information under its privacy notice. OpenAI processes the text sent by the service and may retain prompts, results, and technical information under its API data controls. This policy does not promise zero retention or a single deletion deadline across these providers.

Photo themes

When using photo themes, requests to the app service may include an app-generated identifier, search terms or a selected photo identifier. Unsplash supplies images and receives photo queries or download events. Loading remote images exposes connection details to the image host. See the Unsplash privacy policy.

Your choices and deletion

Choose Stop Sharing with AI in Settings > Suggested Intentions to block future AI requests and turn off automatic suggestions. This cannot recall a request already sent. You can edit or remove intentions and profile text, and manage reminders in the app or system notification settings.

Delete Local Data removes intentions, profile and consent information, preferences, feedback, custom themes, widget copies, and reminders from this device. Bundled themes remain. With iCloud sync enabled, record deletions may propagate to your other devices. Device backups and information previously sent to providers follow their own retention policies. Removing the app has the same limits concerning those separate copies. Contact the developer for requests about information processed by the Attunetion service.

Contact and changes

If you contact the developer, the information you provide is used to respond to your request. This policy will be updated when the app's practices change.
