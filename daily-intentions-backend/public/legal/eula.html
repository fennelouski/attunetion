Optional AI suggestions

You can write intentions manually. When you enable AI sharing on this device and request a suggestion, the app sends the information needed for that feature through our hosted service to OpenAI. Depending on the feature, this can include intention text, earlier intentions, dates and profile answers. You can turn sharing off in Settings.

The Terms of Service explain use of the suggestion service. The Privacy Policy describes what is shared, retention and deletion. For questions, visit support.
